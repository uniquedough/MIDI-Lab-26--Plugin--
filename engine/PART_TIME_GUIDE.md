# MIDI Lab v2.3 — Part Time Taste Profiles

## Reference grounding
The 2019 single *Lies in the Eyes of Love* was written/produced by David Loca; its original single duration is 3:16 (Sugarcane Recordings). Contemporary reviews describe a continuous hypnotic synth ripple, synth/drum-machine combination, off-kilter new-wave feel and a saxophone ending. Use these as **directional observations**, not a transcribed riff, melody, tempo, or chord chart.

Broader Part Time recordings range from inexpensive Casio-flavored home-recorded synth pop and eccentric new-wave tracks (*PDA*) to psychedelic/soft-rock material, with more studio-based arrangements on *Spell #6*.

## Profiles
- `parttime_lies_eyes_love`: strongest for rippling synth hooks and melancholy new-wave motor. Give motif M1 a 3–5 note repeated cell, mutate one note every 8 bars; bass shouldn't mirror it.
- `parttime_casio_bedroom`: inexpensive, slightly irregular synth-pop. Use sparse triads/add2, occasional naïve voicings, short processed guitar answers.
- `parttime_afterhours_softrock`: love-lorn soft rock, tremolo/chorus guitar, loose picked bass, electric piano, sentimental inversions.
- `parttime_neon_motor`: brighter danceable new-wave; repeated bass cell, compact synthetics, small section changes without arena-sized drops.

## Special workflow choices
1. Composer engine maps numeric engine weights to role behavior. Texture words (lo-fi, chorus, tape) are **sound-design suggestions**, not encoded audio.
2. Harmony: simple diatonic cells, nostalgic major-sixth and suspended colors, a single off-color turn; voice-leading more important than dense extensions.
3. Rhythm: mechanical kick/snare backbone, soft fills and rhythmic displacement no more often than once every few bars.
4. Keep a 2–4 bar synth ripple as an anchor. Vary filter/voicing in Logic, or transform one onset/note in MIDI.
5. Leave melodic range open for a voice; an unusual sax-like counterline in a coda is optional (not a recreated solo).
6. Program Change / CC0 / CC32 are prohibited.

## Suggested Logic sounds
Narrow pulse/square synth, slightly detuned analog poly, mono synth or picked bass, old drum-machine kit, understated chorus guitar, occasional synthetic sax-like line. Since MIDI contains no audio, create haze with chorus, saturation, light wow/flutter and short dark reverb.

## Originality
Do not reproduce the distinctive synth figure, harmonic sequence, vocal melody, or sax phrase from *Lies in the Eyes of Love*. Reference behaviors only.
