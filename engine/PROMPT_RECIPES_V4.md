# 4.0 `/cook` prompts

These are intended for ChatGPT to interpret as a music brief and translate to the v4 CLI. `--arc`, `--repetition`, and `--instrument-detail` are native v4 options.

`/cook — 94 BPM, key=B minor, 8:00, profile=yl_tender_nocturne, arc=regret,numbness,recognition,acceptance,triumph, repetition=immutable, anchor=synth, instrument_detail=machine, quiet four-note memory motif, no drum variations, build emotional intensity with upper harmonies only.`

`/cook — 120 BPM, key=E minor, 4:00, harmony=yl_tender_nocturne, rhythm=lcd_motor, melody=drums_solo_jonny, arc=yearning,regret,recognition,acceptance,triumph, repetition=microevolution, instrument_detail=expressive, sparse high-register melodic bass, no big instrumental climax.`

`/cook — 136 BPM, key=Eb dorian, 2:30, profile=pink_bedroom_2step, arc=tenderness,unease,recognition,yearning,acceptance, repetition=phase, instrument_detail=natural, plucks in drifting cycles above unchanging drums and synth pulse.`

`/audit — verify immutable loop note-on/off placements, values and durations, plus forbidden MIDI events.`

For a traditional pop/indie arrangement with section changes, use `midilab3.py cook` or describe a normal v3 composition. V4 is a special-purpose addition rather than a replacement.
